# Wanderbook Atlas

An **Heirloom Storybook Atlas** cooperative geography game built for families and curious kids.

Take turns piloting a shared hot-air balloon across 25 world destinations on all 7 continents, pick glowing story routes, adjust each player's opt-in comfort level (`Scout`, `Navigator`, or `Trailblazer`), tap **Ask the Crew** for team huddles, collect illustrated souvenirs, and flip vintage airmail postcards to **Peek Back in Time**!

## Play Live

Open on your Android tablet, laptop, or Google TV browser:

**https://mlenarz1983.github.io/wanderbook-atlas/**

## Living Room TV + Android Tablet Setup

You have two effortless ways to play on your TV:

1. **Jackbox-Style TV + Tablet Mode:**
   - Open the live link in a Google TV browser app (like *BrowseHere* or *TV Bro*) and tap **Open TV Big Screen on This Device**.
   - On your Android tablet, open the same link, tap **Connect Tablet to a TV**, and enter the 4-letter room code (or scan the QR code shown on the TV).
   - Your tablet stays the **Keeper of the Passport** (saving all player profiles, active missions, and collected postcards) and beams the live map, flights, questions, and 3D postcard flips straight onto the TV!
2. **Instant Android Screen Cast:**
   - Swipe down on your Android tablet → **Cast Screen** to your Google TV Streamer and play in Single-Screen mode.
